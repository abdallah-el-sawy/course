About the project

Why ScamShield AI exists

A graduation project prototype exploring how AI-assisted analysis could give people a second opinion before they trust a message, link, or call.

The idea

Most scam-detection tools try to monitor a person's phone in the background — reading messages automatically, which raises real privacy concerns. ScamShield AI takes a different approach: the person decides what to check, and only that content is analyzed.

This site is a frontend prototype built for a graduation project. There is no real AI model or backend behind it — every "analysis" you see is a simulated result designed to demonstrate what the finished product's experience could look like.

Academic prototype only. Not connected to any real detection engine.

What it covers

  • SMS and WhatsApp messages
  • Links and shortened URLs
  • Emails, including sender and subject
  • Screenshots of chats or webpages
  • QR codes
  • Voice and call recordings
